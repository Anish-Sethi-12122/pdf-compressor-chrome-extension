# PDF Compressor

Shrink your PDFs without the hassle and without compromising your privacy.

**PDF Compressor** is a powerful, client-side browser extension that dramatically reduces the file size of your PDF documents directly on your device. Unlike most PDF compression tools that require you to upload your sensitive documents to a remote server, PDF Compressor uses a custom WebAssembly engine to process everything 100% locally in your browser. 

Your files never leave your device. 

## 🚀 Key Features

- **100% Local Processing:** Total privacy. No uploads, no cloud servers, no data harvesting.
- **Three Compression Modes:**
  - **Balanced (Default):** The perfect sweet spot of reduced file size and good visual quality.
  - **Low:** Minimal compression for when you need excellent image quality.
  - **High:** Aggressive compression for when file size is the absolute priority.
- **Intelligent Validation:** The engine automatically verifies that the resulting PDF is structurally valid and actually smaller. If compression isn't worthwhile, your original file is safely kept.
- **Lightning Fast:** Powered by a highly optimized qpdf WebAssembly core, compression happens in milliseconds to seconds depending on the file.
- **Zero Annoyances:** No ads, no CAPTCHAs, no paywalls, and no watermarks. 
- **Zero Permissions:** We respect your browser. The extension requests exactly **0 permissions** (no `storage`, no `<all_urls>`, no `downloads`).

## 🛡️ Privacy by Design

We believe your documents belong to you. 
- **No PDF Data Collection:** The extension never inspects, reads, or transmits your PDF text, metadata, filenames, or images.
- **Opt-In Analytics:** We strictly use opt-in, anonymous analytics to understand general usage (like "Compression successful"). You are prompted on the first launch, and you can easily opt out at any time right from the extension footer.

Perfect for students, professionals, lawyers, and anyone dealing with large scanned documents or image-heavy PDFs who demands absolute privacy.
