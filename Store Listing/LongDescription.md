Shrink your PDFs without the hassle—and without compromising your privacy.

PDF Compressor is a powerful, lightning-fast browser extension that dramatically reduces the file size of your PDF documents directly on your device. 

Tired of shady websites asking you to upload your sensitive tax documents, legal contracts, or personal IDs just to make them small enough to email? We were too. That is exactly why we built PDF Compressor. 

Unlike other tools, PDF Compressor uses a highly-optimized WebAssembly engine to process everything 100% locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device. No cloud servers, no waiting in line, and absolute zero risk of data leaks.

⭐⭐⭐ WHY CHOOSE PDF COMPRESSOR? ⭐⭐⭐

🔒 UNCOMPROMISING PRIVACY (100% LOCAL)
Your documents are yours. We never upload your files, read your text, or harvest your data. The entire compression process happens securely on your own machine. We don't even require an internet connection to compress files!

⚡ BLAZING FAST WEBASSEMBLY ENGINE
Powered by a custom-compiled qpdf core, compression happens in milliseconds. Don't wait for large files to upload and download—shrink them instantly.

🎛️ THREE PRECISION MODES
Tailor the compression to your exact needs:
• Balanced (Default): The perfect sweet spot. Greatly reduces file size while maintaining highly readable documents and good image quality.
• Low: Minimal compression. Perfect for when you need to retain excellent, crisp visual quality for printing or presentations.
• High: Aggressive compression. When you urgently need to bypass a strict email attachment limit and file size is the only thing that matters.

🛡️ ZERO ANNOYANCES & ZERO PERMISSIONS
We hate bloated software. 
• No Ads
• No CAPTCHAs
• No Paywalls or Premium subscriptions
• No Watermarks on your documents
• No excessive browser permissions (we require literally 0 invasive permissions)

✅ SMART VALIDATION
Our engine is smart. It automatically verifies that the resulting PDF is structurally intact. If a file is already heavily compressed and our engine can't make it smaller, we immediately let you know rather than wasting your time or degrading quality for nothing.
