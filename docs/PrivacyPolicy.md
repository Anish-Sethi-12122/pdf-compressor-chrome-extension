# Privacy Policy for PDF Compressor

*Last Updated: September 2026*

Your privacy is our absolute priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the minimal data handled by the **PDF Compressor** Chrome Extension and our strict commitment to local-only processing.

## 1. Local Document Processing
PDF Compressor is explicitly designed as a local-first utility. **We do not upload, transmit, or store your PDF files.**
- All PDF compression, inspection, and modification happens entirely within your web browser on your local device.
- We do not have servers that process your PDFs.
- No PDF contents, filenames, file sizes, or metadata ever leave your computer.

## 2. Anonymous Usage Analytics (Opt-In)
To help us improve the extension, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) via the Measurement Protocol to collect basic, anonymous usage events (such as "Extension Opened", "Compression Started", or "Error Encountered").
- **Strictly Opt-In:** Analytics are completely disabled by default. You will be asked for consent the first time you open the extension. 
- **Easy Opt-Out:** You can revoke your consent at any time using the toggle in the extension's footer.
- **No Personal Data:** If you opt in, we only track interface interactions. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII), IP addresses, or document-specific information.

## 3. Required Permissions
The extension is built to operate with **0 required browser permissions**. We do not ask for access to your browsing history, downloads, storage, or external websites. 

## 4. Third-Party Services
The only third-party service integrated is Google Analytics 4 (if you explicitly opt in). We do not include any third-party advertising SDKs, trackers, or CAPTCHA services.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support links provided in the Chrome Web Store listing.
